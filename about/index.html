About

State Capacity AI publishes methods, tools, and field guides for AI in the civil stack: governments, NGOs, foundations, and the organizations that hold societies together.

We focus on the decision layer: authority, judgment, review, appeal.

Progress that leaves people behind isn't progress. Everything here is built for real-world deployment. Templates are CC0. Use them, adapt them, build on them.
