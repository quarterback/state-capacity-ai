About

State Capacity AI is a research and strategy practice for AI in the civil stack: governments, NGOs, foundations, and the organizations that hold societies together.

Progress that leaves people behind isn't progress. We work with people worldwide who want to move forward without engineering trust out of the system.

What we publish

Field guides for disclosure, decision classification, practice standards, agent interfaces, and licensing. Everything here is designed for real-world deployment under real-world constraints. The templates are CC0. Use them, adapt them, build on them.
