About

State Capacity AI is a research lab for AI in the civil stack: governments, NGOs, foundations, and the organizations that hold societies together.

We publish methods, tools, and field guides for people deploying AI in decision-bearing systems. The focus is the decision layer: authority, judgment, review, appeal.

Progress means everyone moves. Everything here is built for real-world deployment. Templates are CC0. Use them, adapt them, build on them.
