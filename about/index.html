About

State Capacity AI is an independent project. We build practical resources for people using AI in public services.

The guides on this site cover disclosure standards, decision-grade classification, practice standards for agency teams and vendors, agent interface specifications, and licensing frameworks for shared work.

Everything here is designed for real-world deployment under real-world constraints. The templates are public domain (CC0). Use them, adapt them, build on them.
