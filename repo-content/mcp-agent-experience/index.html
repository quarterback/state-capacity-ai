Transforming Public Service Delivery

Agent-mediated public services represent the future of government interaction. As AI agents become integral to how citizens access services, we need clear standards to ensure consistent, accessible, and trustworthy experiences.

These standards provide a comprehensive framework for public sector organizations to design, implement, and maintain agent-mediated services that serve all citizens effectively. Our approach emphasizes accessibility, security, transparency, and user-centered design principles.

Key Focus Areas

Accessibility First

Ensuring agent experiences meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards and serve diverse populations, including those with disabilities, limited digital literacy, or language barriers.

Security & Privacy

Protecting citizen data through robust authentication, encryption, and privacy-preserving practices that build trust in government services.

Transparency

Why These Standards Matter

Government services must be accessible to all citizens. As agent-mediated interactions become commonplace, we need consistent standards to:

  • Reduce friction in accessing essential public services
  • Build trust through transparent and reliable agent interactions
  • Ensure equity by serving diverse populations with varying needs
  • Enable innovation while maintaining security and compliance
  • Facilitate collaboration across agencies and jurisdictions
  • Improve efficiency for both citizens and government operations

Benefits for Stakeholders

For Government Agencies

  • Reduce development costs through standardized approaches
  • Improve service delivery efficiency and citizen satisfaction
  • Ensure compliance with accessibility and security requirements
  • Enable easier integration across systems and departments

For Citizens

  • Consistent, predictable experiences across different services
  • Accessible interactions regardless of technical proficiency
  • Secure handling of personal information
  • Transparent understanding of how agents work

For Technology Providers

  • Clear requirements for building compliant solutions
  • Reduced integration complexity with government systems
  • Larger addressable market through standardization
  • Framework for demonstrating compliance and quality
