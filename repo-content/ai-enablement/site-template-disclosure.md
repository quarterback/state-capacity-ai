# AI Disclosure Statement (Template Page)
## (https://digital-corps-pdx.github.io/AI-enablement/templates/disclosure.html)

---

For decision-grade systems where automation informs or constrains outcomes.

## Template

- **Summary:** Why this system exists
- **Scope:** What decisions it informs
- **Model Inputs:** What data are used
- **Model Limits:** Known weaknesses or exclusions
- **Human Oversight:** Review and escalation process
- **Accountability:** Responsible team or agency contact

## Example

> **AI Disclosure: Fraud Risk Scoring Tool**
> **Purpose:** Supports fraud detection in unemployment claims.
> **Scope:** Flags high-risk claims for review; does not determine eligibility.
> **Inputs:** Claimant history, employer reports, wage data.
> **Limits:** Does not account for late employer reporting; bias possible in wage history.
> **Oversight:** Monthly review; annual independent audit.
> **Accountability:** transparency@labor.gov
