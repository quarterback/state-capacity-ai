# AI Practice Standards

A short internal charter for teams deploying AI systems in public contexts.

### Example Structure

1. **Human Accountability**
   Every automated system must preserve a human appeal path.

2. **Explainability**
   Systems must be documented in plain language and disclosed publicly.

3. **Proportionality**
   Automation must be justified by clear administrative value.

4. **Continuous Audit**
   Monitor and publish accuracy, bias, and drift quarterly.

5. **Contestability**
   Individuals affected by automated outputs can seek correction without penalty.
