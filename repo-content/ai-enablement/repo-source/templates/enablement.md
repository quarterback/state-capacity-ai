# AI Enablement Statement

A concise, public-facing disclosure for any service using automation in decision-making.


### Example

> **Service:** Medicaid Eligibility Pre-Screening
> **AI Role:** Reviews applications for completeness; flags missing documentation
> **Human Role:** All final determinations made by caseworkers
> **Contestability:** Manual review available at any stage
> **Oversight:** Outputs audited quarterly; metrics published publicly
> **Error Reporting:** ai-issues@statehealth.gov
> **Contact:** Office of Health Modernization
