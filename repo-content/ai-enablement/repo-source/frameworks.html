Why these exist

Public services now rely on automation for scale and speed. People deserve to know when an automated step affects their case. Staff deserve clear limits so they do not carry blame for decisions they did not make. These templates set those lines in plain language.

Levels of authority

Advisory gives a hint and staff decide. Assistive finishes routine steps and staff handle exceptions. Determinative makes a binding choice and appeals are a right. The stronger the authority, the stronger the disclosure.
