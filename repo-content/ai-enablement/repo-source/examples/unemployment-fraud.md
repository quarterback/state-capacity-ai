# AI Enablement Statement

**Service:** Unemployment Insurance Fraud Detection
**Agency:** [State] Employment Security Department
**Last Updated:** October 2025

## What This System Does
Risk scoring tool that flags unemployment insurance claims for potential fraud investigation based on pattern analysis.

## AI's Role
**Advisory Only:** System generates risk scores (0-100) based on:
- Application patterns (timing, frequency, IP address)
- Data inconsistencies across state systems
- Historical fraud indicators in similar claims

**NOT Determinative:** Risk score alone cannot deny benefits or trigger penalties.

## Human Decision Authority
- All claims with risk scores above 70 reviewed by fraud investigator
- Investigator must document independent evidence before fraud determination
- Claimants receive written notice if claim is under fraud review
- No benefit denials or overpayment assessments based solely on risk score

## How to Challenge a Determination
If your claim is flagged for fraud review:
- You will be notified in writing within 3 business days
- You have right to provide additional documentation before any determination
- All fraud determinations must be supported by investigator findings, not system score
- Appeal rights preserved; system involvement disclosed in appeal documentation

## System Oversight
- Independent audit every 6 months by [State Auditor]
- False positive rate published quarterly (target: <5%)
- Demographic bias testing annually
- System automatically disabled if false positive rate exceeds 10% in any month

## System Limitations
- Cannot access criminal records or cross-state data without authorization
- Does not analyze reasons for job separation (human review required)
- Cannot determine claimant intent or knowledge
- Risk score expires after 30 days; re-evaluation required

## Safeguards Learned from Prior Failures
In response to [Previous State] MiDAS system failures (93% false positive rate):
- No automated denials or penalties
- Mandatory human review for all flagged claims
- Claimant notification before investigation begins
- Regular accuracy auditing with published results
