# AI Enablement Statement

**Service:** Child Welfare Hotline Screening
**Agency:** [County] Department of Human Services
**Last Updated:** October 2025

## What This System Does
Risk assessment tool that generates scores for incoming child welfare hotline calls to help screeners prioritize response.

## AI's Role
**Risk Scoring (0-20 scale):** System generates score based on:
- Prior CPS history (this family and historical similar cases)
- Age of children involved
- Reported allegation type
- Environmental risk factors (housing instability, prior substantiated reports)

**Mandatory Investigation Trigger:** Scores above 18 automatically require face-to-face investigation within 24 hours per state law.

**Below 18:** The information summarized by the score does not replace clinical judgment - screener makes determination based on professional assessment and available information.

## Human Decision Authority
- Screeners review all calls; score is one input among many
- Screeners can upgrade response level regardless of score based on call information
- Screeners document rationale for decisions that differ from score recommendation
- Supervisory review required for any decision to screen out a call scored above 15

## How This Affects Families
If you contact our hotline:
- Screener will assess your situation using professional judgment
- High-risk situations trigger automatic investigation (scores 18-20)
- Medium-risk situations (scores 10-17) assessed by screener with supervisor consultation
- You will be informed of next steps before call ends
- Score is not shared with you, but decision and reasoning is explained

## Contestability
- If you disagree with screening decision, request supervisor review during call
- Request administrative review within 5 business days: [contact info]
- Score and screener notes available through records request process
- Legal aid resources available at [URL]

## System Oversight
- Weekly calibration sessions: screeners review scored calls and discuss decision-making
- Quarterly bias audit across race, zip code, reporter type
- Annual recalibration using recent case outcomes
- Community advisory board reviews aggregate data and provides feedback
- Published annually: score accuracy, investigation outcomes, demographic patterns

## System Limitations
- Cannot predict future harm with certainty (risk scores are probabilities, not certainties)
- Historical data reflects past system biases; we actively monitor for perpetuation
- Does not account for family strengths or protective factors (screener assessment required)
- Cannot replace screener's professional judgment and direct call assessment
