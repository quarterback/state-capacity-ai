# AI Enablement Statement

**Service:** Medicaid Expansion Eligibility Recertification
**Agency:** [State] Medicaid Program
**Last Updated:** October 2025

## What This System Does
Automated recertification process for Medicaid expansion adults, required every 6 months under federal law (HR1).

## AI's Role

### Ex Parte (Automated) Recertification:
- Database verification of income, employment, address
- Automatic renewal when no changes detected
- Target: 60% of recertifications completed automatically

### Flagging for Manual Process (40% of cases):
- Income changes detected
- Address verification fails
- Data mismatches between systems
- Non-responsive members

## Human Decision Authority
- Eligibility workers review all flagged cases
- Workers determine what additional information is needed
- Workers make final determination
- Supervisors handle complex situations

## Member Implications
- Auto-renewed members receive confirmation and need take no action
- Flagged members receive letters specifying needed information
- 30 days to respond (extensions available for good cause)
- Coverage continues during review

## Challenge Procedures
- Members may request worker calls
- Submit reconsideration requests
- Standard 90-day appeal window
- Free legal assistance available

## Oversight
- Monthly monitoring: auto-renewal rates, procedural termination rates, reinstatement requests
- Quarterly review for unnecessary documentation requests
- Bias testing for disproportionate flagging
- System adjustments based on data

## System Limitations
- Cannot verify all employment (e.g., gig economy, cash wages)
- Cannot assess circumstances of homelessness
- Cannot determine good cause for missed deadlines
- Cannot assess community engagement compliance
- These require specialist/worker review

## Compliance Context
HR1 requires six-month recertification. Financial penalties for states that fail to comply. This system helps meet federal requirements while minimizing burden on members and staff.
