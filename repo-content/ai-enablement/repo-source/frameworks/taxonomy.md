# Decision-Grade AI Taxonomy

Public services use automation at different levels of authority.
Classifying systems this way helps determine oversight needs.

| Level | Name | Description | Examples |
|--------|------|--------------|-----------|
| 1 | Advisory | Suggests or highlights information; human decides | risk scores, checklists |
| 2 | Assistive | Completes routine decisions when all criteria met | auto-approvals, renewals |
| 3 | Determinative | Makes binding decisions; human appeal required | mandatory investigations |

Systems that cross from assistive to determinative require additional public disclosure, audit mechanisms, and human appeal paths.
