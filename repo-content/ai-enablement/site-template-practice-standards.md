Short norms for teams and vendors. Write the guardrails. Work within them.

## Five standards

1. Human accountability and appeal every time
2. Plain language disclosure for decision paths
3. Proportional use matched to risk and value
4. Continuous audit and public reporting
5. Easy contest and no penalty for asking

## Vendor terms

Explainable outputs. Full audit trail. Human override. Test access for third parties. Change notice. Accuracy thresholds with remedies.
