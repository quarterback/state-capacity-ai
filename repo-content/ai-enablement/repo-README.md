# AI Enablement Statements

> Make authority visible.
> Make decisions contestable.
> Make systems worthy of the trust they require.

This repository provides **lightweight templates** for agencies, civic technologists, and public who deploy automation in decision-making systems. The idea is that you'll adapt these to your own needs, purposes and use cases. This is meant to start the conversation, not be an exhaustive set of standards.

## Why this matters

AI systems are entering public services faster than accountability standards can catch up.
This repo helps close that gap with three simple tools:

| Framework | Purpose |
|------------|----------|
| **AI Enablement Statement** | A public-facing notice showing how AI participates in a service |
| **AI Disclosure Statement** | A more detailed transparency brief for decision-grade systems |
| **AI Practice Standards** | Internal guidance for agencies and vendors to align around responsible use |

## Quick Start

1. Copy any template from `/templates`
2. Fill in the sections with your service details
3. Publish to your agency or vendor site under `/ai` or `/transparency`
4. Submit a pull request here to share your example
