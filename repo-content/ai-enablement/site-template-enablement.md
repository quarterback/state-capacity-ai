# AI Enablement Statement (Template Page)
## (https://digital-corps-pdx.github.io/AI-enablement/templates/enablement.html)

---

A one page notice that shows where automation sits in a service, what humans control, and how people can challenge outcomes.

## Template

- **Service** name
- **Agency** owner
- **Purpose and context** why this exists now
- **AI role** what the system does and when
- **Human role** who decides and why
- **Contestability** how a person asks for review
- **Oversight** audits, cadence, triggers
- **Limitations** what it cannot do
- **Data sources** what feeds it
- **Contact** where to report errors

## Why this matters

It gives people a clear view of how decisions are made. It protects workers by stating boundaries. It forces teams to plan oversight before launch.

## Example

**Medicaid renewal six month cycle**

- **Purpose and context** federal change requires more frequent checks. This system reduces paperwork while staff keep control of edge cases.
- **AI role** verify income and address for renewals when sources agree. flag mismatches for worker review.
- **Human role** workers review flags and can override. supervisors look for pattern issues.
- **Contestability** people can request manual review by phone or form. appeals remain unchanged.
- **Oversight** monthly sample audit and quarterly bias review. pause if error rates rise above threshold.
- **Limitations** cannot judge good cause or disability. cannot confirm wages not in reporting systems.
- **Data sources** revenue, employment, postal, dmv, ssa, member docs.
- **Contact** medicaid-ai@state.gov
