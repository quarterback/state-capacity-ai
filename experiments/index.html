Experiments

Working prototypes of the five principles in practice—actual demonstrations of context engineering design applied to real deployment scenarios.

#001 In Progress

Context Legibility Dashboard

A tool for surfacing AI system assumptions, limits, and reasoning in public-facing systems. Designed for government benefits eligibility chatbots and similar high-stakes contexts.

What it demonstrates

Principle 3: Context Legibility. Shows what "nutrition labels for AI" look like in practice—built for oversight, advocacy, and critical moments.

Key features

  • Information sources the system uses and their provenance
  • Assumptions it makes about the user's situation
  • Explicit scope: what the system can and cannot do
  • Step-by-step reasoning for decisions
  • Controls for users to correct wrong assumptions
